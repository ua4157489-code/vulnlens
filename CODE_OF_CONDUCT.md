# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want VulnLens to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Give and accept feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information
- Using this project to plan or promote unauthorized access to systems you do not own or have permission to test

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may block participants who violate this code. To report a concern, contact the maintainer [@ua4157489-code](https://github.com/ua4157489-code) through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
